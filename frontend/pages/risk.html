Risk Disclosure

Draft – to be reviewed by a qualified Indian securities-law professional before launch.

Disclaimer: We are not SEBI registered. Content is for information and education only. Do your own research.

General

You can lose some or all of your invested money. Never invest money you cannot afford to lose.

Equity markets

Prices can fall sharply due to company, sector, economic or global events.

Derivatives, futures and options

These are leveraged and complex; losses can exceed the amount initially invested.

Intraday trading

Rapid price movement and costs make intraday trading high risk.

Commodities, gold and silver

Prices are affected by global demand, currency and policy changes.

Cryptocurrency

Highly volatile, may be unregulated in your jurisdiction, and can lose value rapidly.

Volatility, leverage, liquidity

Volatility can cause sudden losses, leverage magnifies losses, and low liquidity may stop you from exiting a position.
