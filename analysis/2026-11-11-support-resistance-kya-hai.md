## Support kya hai?
Support wo price area hota hai jahan pehle girta hua price ruka ya palta tha, kyunki us level par kharidne ki ruchi zyada thi.

## Resistance kya hai?
Resistance wo price area hota hai jahan pehle badhta hua price ruka ya palta tha, kyunki us level par bechne ka dabav zyada tha.

## Chart par kaise dekhein?
- Pichhle kuch highs aur lows ko ek line se jodein.
- Jitni baar price kisi level se palta hai, wo level utna zyada dekha jata hai.
- Volume ke saath dekhein, taaki pata chale us level par kitni activity thi.

## Dhyan rakhne wali baatein
- Ye levels guarantee nahi hain; price inhe tod sakta hai.
- Alag timeframe par alag levels dikhte hain.
- Sirf ek indicator ke bharose faisla na lein.
